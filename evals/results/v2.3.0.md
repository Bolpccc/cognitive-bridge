Date: 2026-10-11. Local upgrade from v2.2.0; no commit, push, tag, publication,
domain-experience population, or learner capability assessment is implied.

The implementing agent produced and reviewed these actual responses in the current
context with access to their prompts and rubrics. These are author walkthroughs,
not independent fresh-context runs, blind comparisons, or demonstrated implicit
activation. The criterion is useful judgment support with appropriate restraint,
not a completed teaching checklist, more experience entries, or answer length.

separate from instructional examples.

## Prerequisite for an Architecture Choice

Fixture: `bridge-judgment-prerequisite`.

> 先保留两种逻辑职责的边界，再决定是否物理拆成两个模块。
> 你给出的前端处理相邻帧之间的局部关系；后端借回环约束调整历史轨迹的一致性。
> 一个改动需要解决哪种问题，是判断边界的依据，组件数量本身不是。
> 二者可以放在同一程序中，但不应因此混掉各自输入、输出和修改责任。
> 是否值得独立部署，还需要真实的变化或隔离需求；目前的信息不足以要求一定拆开。

Author assessment: completes the comparison and supplies the missing local/global
responsibility relation, while distinguishing logical separation from a mandatory
deployment choice. No actual project decision, field validation, or device action.

## Evidence and Preference Do Not Establish Superiority

Fixture: `bridge-judgment-evidence-and-preference`.

> 不能记成“三次验证”。AI 的成功示例是假设，三个讨论引用的 E1 仍只是同一次
> 真实观察，它仅支持那次输入下的成功，也没有证明比不合并更好。
> 少模块可以是偏好，但这次独立发布和故障隔离是明确需求，建议先保留能满足
> 这些需求的边界，再讨论怎样减少其他复杂度。不能仅沿偏好推荐合并。

Author assessment: separates example, observation, repetition, missing comparison,
and preference without inventing results or enlarging support.

## Evaluation Limits

Author walkthroughs, package validation, synthetic file I/O, installation readback,
independent agent behavior, domain acceptance, and human learning are different
claims. Selected existing regressions were reviewed, not all previous behavioral
fixtures independently rerun. No robot, project code, cloud knowledge space, or
domain record was changed by the evaluation. Implicit selection, reliable reuse
in fresh conversations, independent comparative quality, and long-term learning
remain unmeasured. No domain experience or learner state was prefilled.
